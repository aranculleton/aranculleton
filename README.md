# Aran Culleton

I'm a Data Science Consultant at xantura (Newton Europe), previously at British Airways and the BBC.
I work across analytics engineering, applied machine learning, forecasting, and risk decisioning.

Most of my work sits where data science meets operations: SQL/Python pipelines,
model-ready datasets, validation checks, scoring systems, and dashboards people actually use.
Recent work has included prioritization models over 1.5M+ records and ingestion/QA workflows
that reduced data-prep cycles from weeks to days.

Right now I'm building [CaseSignal](https://github.com/aranculleton/casesignal),
a synthetic account-risk scoring project that combines structured account behavior
with AI-extracted note signals to rank cases, explain risk movement, and suggest next actions.

I'm also contributing to [CopulAX](https://github.com/aranculleton/copulax),
a JAX-based copula modeling library, where I'm working on distribution support,
tests, and contributor documentation (including clearer macOS/Linux setup guidance).

Outside work, I'm also a chef.
I spend most of my off-hours cooking, traveling, watching films,
and logging them on [Letterboxd](https://letterboxd.com/ar9n/).
